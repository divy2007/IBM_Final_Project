### Slide 2: The Problem – The Invisible Crisis in Indian Messes
* **Slide Title:** The Problem: Institutional Food Waste Scale in India
* **Visual Layout:** Split screen. On the left: Indian food waste statistics (68 Million tons annual loss, ₹1.5 Lakh Cr financial drain). On the right: Photo of a busy university mess bin with an overlay reading *"Invisible Loss: Which dishes were wasted today?"*
* **Slide Content:**
  * **Massive Volume:** Indian university messes (IITs, NITs, Central Varsities) & IT parks discard 25%–35% of prepared food daily.
  * **The Data Blindspot:** Mess wardens log total weight manually, but have **zero visibility** into *which specific dishes* (Paneer vs. Rice vs. Dal) are wasted.
  * **Compounding Costs:** High ingredient spend (Paneer, Ghee, Proteins), wasted labor, and rotting organic waste generating landfill methane.
  * **Existing Alternatives Fail:** Manual registers are inaccurate; basic weigh-scales fail to classify regional Indian dishes.
* **Speaker Talking Points:**
  > "Institutional messes in India serve thousands of meals daily. However, mess managers face a major blindspot: they know how much total weight is thrown away, but they have no idea whether that weight was expensive Paneer Butter Masala, Steamed Basmati Rice, or Dal. Without itemized data, catering contractors re-order ingredients that students or employees consistently reject. Manual logging during rush hour is impossible, and basic scales fail to identify regional Indian recipes."

### Slide 5: The Autonomous Agent Workflow (Zero Human Intervention)
* **Slide Title:** Autonomous Agent Workflow in Action
* **Visual Layout:** Sequential flowchart tracking data transformation from bin disposal to automated order report.
* **Slide Content:**
  * **Step 1 (Vision Agent):** Detects discarded dish (e.g., 350g Paneer Butter Masala), tags category, calculates ₹126 loss.
  * **Step 2 (Analytics Agent):** Detects pattern—Paneer waste up 38% on Thursdays when served alongside specific rice items.
  * **Step 3 (Procurement Agent):** Computes precise recipe reduction (-18% prep) and generates draft purchase orders in INR.
  * **Step 4 (Chef Notification):** Sends instant push alert (WhatsApp/Slack) with one-click approval for inventory adjustments.
* **Speaker Talking Points:**
  > "Let's walk through our autonomous agent pipeline. When a plate is scraped, our Vision Agent tags the specific Indian dish and logs its mass and monetary loss in Rupees. The Analytics Agent aggregates these entries against historical trends, weather, and university exam schedules to spot hidden anomalies. Finally, our Procurement Agent calculates exact batch reductions and auto-drafts purchasing orders in INR."
